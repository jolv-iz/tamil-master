1. Grammar

1.1 Sentence Structure

Subject → [Time] → Object → Verb — prepositions always attach after the word (post-position)
Question word sits in the middle; swap only the sentence completion to change tense:
Neenga eppadi irukeenga? → How are you? | Neenga eppadi irundheenga? → How were you?

"Can" changes the pronoun form: Naan → Ennala · Neenga → Unganala · Avaru → Avarunala · Names: Jo → Jo-nala
Time range "from X until Y": irundhu [start] [end] varaikum
Pathionnu mani-la irundhu naalu mani varaikum → From 11 o'clock until 4 o'clock
Repeating an adjective adds strong emphasis or "many big":
Neraiya periya periya malai iruku → There are many very big mountains
Polite imperative: add -nga to the verb base
Poo → Poo-nga (Go / Please go) · Vaango (Come / Please come)
"Even if only a little / May do": konjam [verb]-nalum [verb]-vaen
Konjam reading panninalum pannuvaen → I may/will do a bit of reading even if only a little
"While / When [subject] was there": [verb]-ndha appo
Irundha appo → While you were there · Thoongindha appo → While (he) was sleeping
"Because of being [somewhere]": iruka-naela
Inga iruka-naela → Because of being here
Repeating a question word = asking for a list: yenna yenna = "what else and all"
Vera yenna yenna anupitaanga? → What else and all did she send?
"[X time] ago": [number] [unit] munnadi
10 varsham munnadi → 10 years ago · Rendu maasam munnadi → 2 months ago

1.10a "Only" — Thaan vs Mattum

Thaan — emphasises the subject/object (same, that exact thing)
Aadhu thaan sapuditan. → I ate that (very thing).
Ennoda boyfriend thaan sollan. → Only my boyfriend says it.
Mattum — restricts to that item alone (nothing else)
Coffee mattum venum. → I want coffee only (not tea).

4. Example Sentences

4.1 Location & Being

Naan nalla irukan.
I am good.
Neenga eppadi irukeenga?
How are you?
Avanga yenga irundhaanga?
Where was she?
Amma kitchen-la irukanga.
Mom is in the kitchen.
Amma kitchen-la illa.
Mom is not in the kitchen.
Climate nalla iruku.
The weather is good.
Food nalla irundhuchu.
Food was good.
Singapore oru island.
Singapore is an island.

4.2 Possession & Names

Ennoda Peru Jo. Naan Singapore-la Irukan.
My name is Jo. I am in Singapore.
Enkitta laptop iruku aana charger illa.
I have a laptop but I don't have a charger.
Jo-ku Akka irukaanga.
Jo has an older sister.
Jo-kitta periya laptop iruku.
Jo has a big laptop.
Enaku neraiya work iruku.
I have a lot of work.

4.3 Special Verbs (like, want, know)

Enaku spanish theriyum aana grammar nalla theriyathu.
I know Spanish but don't know the grammar well.
Ungaluku Tamil theriyuma?
Do you know Tamil?
Ammaku ungaloda cooking pudikuma?
Does mom like your cooking?
Ungaluku inaiku rest vendama?
Don't you want rest today?

4.4 Should have / Could have / Let's

Naan nethu Thoongirukanum aana Naan Thoongala.
I should have slept yesterday but I didn't.
Naan ungaloda book thaedirukalam aana Naan Thaedala.
I could have searched for your book but I didn't.
Vaanga, Namma Restaurant-la oru sandwich sapadalam.
Come, let's eat a sandwich at the restaurant.
Vaanga, tamil-la Paesalam.
Come, let's speak in Tamil.

4.5 Verb Sentences

Naan office-la homework pannitan.
I did homework in the office.
Ennoda amma rathiri sapadu-kaga pasta pannuvanga.
My mother will make pasta for dinner.
Ennoda Appa ippo Thoongiraaru.
My father is sleeping now.
Neenga SapudiraAppo, Naan Ammakitta Paesitan.
When you were eating, I was talking to mom.
Amma Story SolliraAppo, Enaku andha story time romba Pudikum.
When mom tells a story, I really like that story time.
Jo-nala 10 meters maela yaera mudiyuma?
Can Jo climb more than 10 meters?
Joku 10th floor-ku lift-la yaeranuma? Illa? Steps-la yaeranuma?
Does Jo need to go up to the 10th floor by lift? Or by steps?
Jo tree-maela yaerikanum yaena tree-maela oru fruit iruku.
Jo should climb the tree because there's a fruit on it.
Neenga bus-la yaerinathuku apram ticket vaangunga.
After you got on the bus, buy a ticket.
Namma nethu bus-la yaerirkalam yaena bus-la yaarumae illa.
We could have taken the bus yesterday because there was no one on it.
Neenga lunch sapudalaiya?
Did you have lunch?
Ennoda boyfriend enna sapuditaaru, Naan aadhu thaan sapuditan.
My boyfriend ate something, I ate that same thing.
Ungaluku pasikutha?
Are you hungry?
Enaku pasikala.
I'm not hungry.
Yaen pasikala?
Why not hungry?
Yaena ennoda breakfast heavy irundhuchu athunaela ippo pasikala, apram pasikum.
Because my breakfast was heavy, so not hungry now, will get hungry later.
Jo'ku pasi vandha, eppadi react pannuveenga?
When Jo gets hungry, how will you react?
Jo'ku lunch-kaga dhinamum ethana maniku pasikum?
Every day at what time does Jo get hungry for lunch?
Jo'nala pasi evlo thaanga mudiyum?
How much hunger can Jo bear?
Enaku romba velai, illa, romba fun iruku-na, naan romba pasi thaanguvaen.
When I'm very busy, or having a lot of fun, I can bear much hunger.

4.6 Postpositions in Context

Naan red dress-la nalla irupan.
I will look good in a red dress.
Enaku coffee sugar-illama venum.
I want coffee without sugar.
Sapadu table maela iruku.
The food is on the table.
Cat house pinnadi iruku.
The cat is behind the house.
Amma table keezhla irukaanga.
Mom is under the table.

4.7 Longer Constructed Sentences

Ennoda Peru Jo, Naan Singapore-la Irukan. Naan Hongkong-la Irundhan. Ennoda Amma Appa china-la irukaanga.
My name is Jo, I am in Singapore. I was in Hongkong. My parents are in China.
Appa'oda Ammaku leg pain irundhuchu athunaela Avanga ippo hospital-la irukaanga.
Dad's mom had leg pain; because of that she is now at the hospital.
Enaku blue color-la shirt pudikum aana Enaku ippo shirt vendam yaena Enkitta neraiya shirts already iruku.
I like blue shirts but I don't want one now because I already have many.
Naan ennoda jannal-kitta irukanum.
I want to be near my window.
Naan ennoda Singapore trip-la Jo'oda veetla thanguvaen.
I will stay at Jo's house during my Singapore trip.
